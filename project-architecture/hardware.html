🔌 Hardware Components

Field Device Inventory

All components selected for low cost, high reliability, and agricultural environment resilience.

🖥️
Microcontroller
ESP32-WROOM-32

Dual-core 240 MHz processor with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Handles sensor polling, local logic, and MQTT/LoRa communication.

  • Dual-core Xtensa LX6 @ 240 MHz
  • 520 KB SRAM, 4 MB Flash
  • Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n + BT 4.2
  • 34 programmable GPIO pins
  • 12-bit ADC × 18 channels
💧
Soil Moisture
Capacitive v1.2

Capacitive design avoids electrolysis corrosion. Measures volumetric water content (VWC) via dielectric permittivity of soil.

  • Operating voltage: 3.3 – 5.5 V
  • Output: 0 – 3.0 V analog
  • No corrosion (no exposed metal)
  • Accuracy: ±2% VWC typical
⚗️
EC Sensor
DFRobot SEN0244

Measures electrical conductivity of soil solution to assess nutrient concentration and trigger fertigation dosing decisions.

  • Range: 0 – 20 mS/cm
  • Resolution: 0.01 mS/cm
  • Operating temp: 5 – 40°C
  • Analog voltage output
🌡️
Temp & Humidity
DHT22 / AM2302

Captures microclimate data used to calculate Growing Degree Days (GDD) for the pest forecasting model.

  • Temp range: −40 to +80°C (±0.5°C)
  • Humidity: 0–100% RH (±2–5%)
  • Single-wire digital protocol
  • 2-second sampling interval
🌊
Flow Meter
YF-S201 Hall-Effect

Tracks precise water volume delivered per irrigation event, enabling feedback verification of actuator commands.

  • Flow range: 1 – 30 L/min
  • Pulse factor: 7.5 pulses/litre
  • Working pressure: ≤1.75 MPa
  • Hall-effect output (digital)
⚡
Solid-State Relay
SSR-25DA (25A/250V)

Zero-crossing SSR switches irrigation valves, fertigation pumps, and spray nozzles. No mechanical wear, faster switching than electromechanical relays.

  • Input: 3–32 V DC (ESP32 compatible)
  • Output: 24–380 V AC, 25A max
  • Zero-crossing switching
  • Optical isolation for safety
🔌 Wiring Topology

ESP32 Pin Mapping

Reference wiring plan for each sensor and actuator to the ESP32 GPIO pins.

// ESP32 Field Node — Pin Assignment SENSORS: GPIO 34 → Soil Moisture Sensor (Analog ADC1_CH6) GPIO 35 → EC Sensor (Analog ADC1_CH7) GPIO 4 → DHT22 Temp/Humidity (Digital one-wire) GPIO 18 → Flow Meter (Pulse) (Interrupt-driven counter) ACTUATORS (via SSR): GPIO 25 → Irrigation Valve SSR (HIGH = open valve) GPIO 26 → Fertigation Pump SSR (HIGH = run pump) GPIO 27 → Spray Nozzle SSR (HIGH = spray) COMMUNICATION: Built-in → Wi-Fi (MQTT over TLS) GPIO 16 → LoRa TX (SX1276) (UART2) GPIO 17 → LoRa RX (SX1276) (UART2) POWER: 3.3 V → Sensors (VCC rails) 5.0 V → SSR input signal (via level shifter) Note: SSR output switches mains AC — isolate carefully!
